Workout Hero

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Workout Hero ("Workout Hero", "we", "us") handles information in the Workout Hero app for iOS and Android. It describes what data the app uses, where that data is stored, and which third parties receive any of it.

Summary

  • Workout Hero has no user accounts and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number in the app.
  • We do not operate our own server. Your workouts, health data, goals, notes and workout routes are stored on your device, in Apple Health or Health Connect, and, on iOS, in your personal iCloud account.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share health data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Some data does leave your device. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. On iOS, app-usage analytics are sent to Amplitude. These services are described below.

1. Workout and health data stored on your device

Workout Hero shows your workouts in a calendar, statistics, goals and widgets. To do this, the app works with:

  • workouts: type, start and end time, duration, distance, active calories and average heart rate;
  • goals, workout schedules and reminders that you create;
  • notes you add to workouts;
  • custom workout types, colors, filters and app settings.

On iOS, your workouts are stored in Apple Health and read from there. Goals, notes, schedules and settings are stored on your device. Heart-rate values the app has read from Apple Health are cached on your device to speed up loading.

On Android, workouts are stored in a local database on your device. Goals, notes, schedules and settings are also stored locally.

We do not send this data to any server that we operate. It is not included in our analytics, except as described in Section 5.

2. Apple Health (iOS) and Health Connect (Android)

Apple Health. With your permission, Workout Hero reads workouts, workout routes, heart rate, active energy, steps, walking and running distance, and body mass from Apple Health. It uses this data to display and analyze your workouts. Workouts you create or record in the app, including their distance, calories and routes, are saved to Apple Health. You can review or revoke these permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Health Connect. On Android, Health Connect is optional. You can use Workout Hero without connecting it. If you do connect it, the app can read exercise sessions, distance, active and total calories burned, heart rate and routes that are available to it, including historical data. It can also write your Workout Hero workouts, with their distance and active calories, to Health Connect. You can turn Health Connect sync off in the app, and you can review or revoke permissions at any time in Health Connect settings.

Data you share with Apple Health or Health Connect is stored and managed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Workout Hero does not send health data from Apple Health or Health Connect to us or to any third party. It is not used for advertising or marketing.

3. Live Workout, location and routes

Live Workout lets you record a workout in real time. With your permission, the app uses:

  • Location (GPS), only while a Live Workout is being recorded, to measure distance, pace, speed and elevation and to record your route. On iOS, if you allow background location, tracking continues while your screen is locked. On Android, a foreground notification is shown while tracking is active.
  • Motion and barometer data (iOS) and physical activity recognition (Android) during a Live Workout, for example to measure elevation gain.
  • Heart rate (iOS), read from Apple Health during a Live Workout when available, for example from a paired Apple Watch or heart-rate monitor.

Location is not used when no Live Workout is running, and it is not sent to us or to any third party.

On iOS, the route is saved to Apple Health together with the workout. Until that save completes, a temporary copy is kept on your device so a route is not lost if the app is interrupted. The copy is then removed.

On Android, route points are stored in the app's local database together with the workout. They are not written to Health Connect.

On iOS, an active Live Workout may appear as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

4. iCloud sync (iOS)

On iOS, Workout Hero uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage to keep your goals, workout notes, schedules, custom colors, filters and certain settings in sync across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data is stored in your own iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it. If iCloud is not available or is turned off, the app stores this data only on your device.

Workouts themselves are not stored in iCloud by Workout Hero. They stay in Apple Health, which Apple may sync using its own settings.

On Android, the operating system may include app data in your device backup to your Google account, depending on your device's backup settings. This is controlled by Android and Google, not by Workout Hero.

5. Analytics (iOS only)

The iOS app uses Amplitude to understand how the app is used and to improve it. The Android app does not include any analytics SDK.

On iOS, the app sends Amplitude usage events and usage properties, for example:

  • app opened or closed, screens viewed, onboarding steps and buttons tapped;
  • that a workout, Live Workout or goal was created, edited, finished or deleted, including the workout type, goal type and duration;
  • whether Apple Health or notification permission was granted;
  • paywall views and purchase steps, product identifier and whether you have a subscription;
  • app settings, app version, iOS version, device model, number of workouts and goals, days since install, number of sessions and time spent in the app;
  • error messages when something in the app fails.

Amplitude also assigns a random device identifier and, like any internet service, receives your IP address when data is sent. Amplitude may use it to estimate an approximate location, such as country or city. We also use Amplitude Experiment to turn some app features on or off remotely.

We do not send your name, email address, workout notes, heart-rate values, workout routes or precise location to Amplitude. We do not use Amplitude data for advertising or cross-app tracking. For more information, see Amplitude's Privacy Notice.

6. Crash reporting

Workout Hero does not include a third-party crash-reporting SDK. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may provide us with anonymous crash reports according to your device settings and their policies. On iOS, error messages may also be included in the analytics described in Section 5.

7. Subscriptions and purchases

Workout Hero offers optional paid features (Workout Hero PRO). Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store In-App Purchase) on iOS and Google (Google Play Billing) on Android. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and to check whether you have access to paid features.

To do this, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user identifier generated by RevenueCat (not your name or email);
  • purchase and subscription information from Apple or Google, such as product, purchase date, renewal and expiration status, and transaction identifiers;
  • basic technical information, such as platform, app version, device language and country or storefront, and your IP address as part of the connection.

We use this information only to provide and restore your subscription and to understand subscription performance. We never receive or store your payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Workout Hero shows reminders, goal-progress messages and workout notifications. These notifications are created and scheduled on your device. We do not use a push-notification server and do not collect a push token. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.

9. Widgets

Workout Hero widgets show your calendar, goals and progress on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. To do this, the app stores a summary of this data on your device where the widget can read it. Widget data is not sent anywhere. Note that anyone who can see your screen can see what a widget displays.

10. What leaves your device

  • To Apple Health / Health Connect (on your device, managed by Apple or Google): workouts and related data you choose to save or sync.
  • To your iCloud account (iOS): goals, notes, schedules and settings, as described in Section 4.
  • To Apple, Google and RevenueCat: purchase and subscription information, as described in Section 7.
  • To Amplitude (iOS only): usage analytics, as described in Section 5.

Other than these, Workout Hero does not transmit your data. We do not operate a backend server, do not sell personal data, and do not share it with advertisers.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Deleting a workout on iOS deletes it from Apple Health. iOS only allows an app to delete workouts it created. Workouts from other apps or devices must be deleted in the Health app or in their source app.
  • Deleting a workout on Android removes it from Workout Hero. If Health Connect sync is on and the workout was created by Workout Hero, the copy in Health Connect is also deleted. If sync is off, it is deleted the next time sync is turned on. Workouts that came from other apps must be deleted in Health Connect or in their source app.
  • Deleting goals or notes removes them from the app and, on iOS, from iCloud sync.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally by Workout Hero, including local workouts and routes on Android. It does not remove data saved in Apple Health, Health Connect or iCloud. You can manage that data in the Health app, Health Connect or your iCloud settings.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for billing, subscription and legal purposes, even if you delete the app.
  • Analytics data (iOS) is kept by Amplitude according to our data-retention settings with Amplitude.

Because we do not have accounts and cannot identify you by name, we usually cannot look up your data. If you want us to request deletion of analytics or subscription data, contact us. It helps if you can provide details such as the RevenueCat customer ID shown in the app or your purchase receipt.

12. Your rights and choices

You can control what Workout Hero can access at any time. Use your device settings to manage Health, Health Connect, location, motion, notification and iCloud permissions. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

13. Children

Workout Hero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
