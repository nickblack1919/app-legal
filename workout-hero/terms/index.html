iOS — Apple Health. On iOS, Workout Hero works with Apple Health (HealthKit). With your permission, it reads workouts and related health data, such as heart rate, energy, distance and routes, and saves workouts you create or record in the Application. Android — Health Connect. On Android, Health Connect is optional. You can use Workout Hero without connecting it. 6. Live Workout, Location and Routes

Live Workout lets you record a workout in real time. When you start a Live Workout and grant permission, the Application may use your device's location (GPS) and motion sensors. It uses them to measure distance, pace, speed and elevation and to record your route. Location is used for workouts you start, not for continuous tracking at other times. On some devices, tracking can continue with the screen locked while a Live Workout is in progress.

GPS and sensor accuracy varies with your device, environment, signal quality and other factors. Distance, pace, speed, elevation, heart rate, calories and routes may be incomplete or inaccurate.
